Report status as a structured field instead of parsing it from the answer's first characters.

Baseline: 3 of 6 unanswerable attempts put an explanatory paragraph before `Not found in the Atlas corpus:`, so the prefix parser classified them as answered even though the content was right. v1 (a stricter prompt instruction) did not move this at all: status 92% in both, and the two unanswerable cases just traded failures. The model doesn't reliably place a text marker, so status shouldn't depend on one.

Change: every request sets `output_config.format` to a JSON schema `{status: "answered" | "not_found", answer: string}`. Tool-calling turns are unaffected; the final answer text is constrained to that shape, including on the forced final turn after the 3-round cap (`tool_choice: "none"`), which I checked with a live probe before building. The prompt's not-found section now says to set status instead of writing a prefix, and the CLI adds the prefix for display. A moved-paragraph heuristic was considered and rejected: it can't tell "explanation, then not found" from a partial answer whose last paragraph covers the part the corpus doesn't answer. The grader now grades the status the agent reports; its checks and judge are unchanged. v1's prompt change is reverted, so v2 is baseline plus this change only.

# Data Retention & Privacy Policy

**Effective:** June 1, 2024
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose & Regulatory Basis

This policy establishes minimum retention periods and access controls
for underwriting, claims, and applicant data, consistent with
state insurance record-retention statutes and Atlas's contractual
obligations to reinsurers.

## 2. Underwriting File Retention

Underwriting files (applications, inspection reports, correspondence,
and binding documentation) must be retained for **7 years after policy
expiration or cancellation**, whichever is later.

## 3. Claims File Retention

Claim files must be retained for **10 years after claim closure**. If a
claim is subject to litigation, arbitration, or a regulatory inquiry at
the time of closure, the file must be retained until 2 years after
final resolution, if that period extends beyond the standard 10 years.

## 4. PII Handling & Access Controls

- Applicant and claimant personally identifiable information (PII)
  must be stored in encrypted form at rest and in transit.
- Access to PII is limited to underwriting, claims, and compliance staff
  with a documented business need; access is logged and reviewed
  quarterly.

## 5. Data Breach Notification Procedure

Any suspected unauthorized access to PII must be reported to the Atlas
Compliance Officer within 24 hours of discovery. Compliance will
determine notification obligations under applicable state breach
notification statutes within 5 business days.

# Claims Handling Policy

**Effective:** January 15, 2024
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs claim intake, investigation, reserving, and
litigation referral standards for all lines of business written by
Atlas Underwriting.

## 2. First Notice of Loss (FNOL) Timelines

- All claims must be logged in the claims system within **2 business
  days** of first notice.
- Claims involving bodily injury or a coverage dispute must be assigned
  to an adjuster within **1 business day** of logging.

## 3. Claim Investigation Standards

- Initial contact with the insured or claimant must occur within 3
  business days of assignment.
- A written investigation plan is required for any claim with an
  initial reserve above $50,000.

## 4. Reserve Setting Guidelines

- Reserves must reflect the adjuster's best estimate of ultimate
  exposure, including indemnity and expense, and must be updated within
  5 business days of any material development.
- Reserve increases above $100,000 require supervisor approval.

## 5. Litigation Referral Thresholds

Claims must be referred to Atlas Legal when any of the following apply:

- Incurred reserve (indemnity + expense) exceeds **$250,000**
- Suit has been filed or a plaintiff's attorney has sent a letter of
  representation
- The claim involves an allegation of bad faith

## 6. File Documentation & Retention

All claim files must contain the FNOL record, investigation notes,
reserve worksheets, correspondence, and final disposition summary.
Claim files are retained per the schedule in the Data Retention &
Privacy Policy; this policy does not independently set retention
periods.

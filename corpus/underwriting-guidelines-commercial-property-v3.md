# Underwriting Guidelines: Commercial Property (v3.0)

**Effective:** November 1, 2025
**Status:** Active. Supersedes v2.0 (effective March 1, 2023) in full
for all business bound, renewed, or endorsed on or after the effective
date above. This revision reflects updated reinsurance treaty terms and
increased wind/hail loss experience in Tier 1 coastal counties.

## 1. Purpose & Scope

These guidelines govern binding authority, documentation requirements,
and referral triggers for commercial property risks written by Atlas
Underwriting field underwriters, occupancy classes 1-6 (per Atlas
occupancy schedule).

## 2. Binding Authority & TIV Limits

### 2.1 Standard Binding Authority

Field underwriters may bind commercial property risks up to a Total
Insured Value (TIV) of **$15,000,000 per location** without home office
referral, provided all conditions in Section 4 are met.

### 2.2 Coastal Wind/Hail Zones (Tier 1 Counties)

For risks located in a Tier 1 coastal wind/hail county (per the Atlas
coastal county schedule), standard binding authority is reduced:

- Maximum TIV: **$8,000,000 per location**
- Minimum named-storm deductible: **5% of TIV**

Risks exceeding this TIV in a Tier 1 county require home office referral
per Section 5.

## 3. Required Documentation

### 3.1 Standard Documentation Package

- Statement of Values (SOV), current within 90 days
- Four-point inspection report, required if the roof is **older than 10
  years** (tightened from 15 years under v2.0)
- Five years of currently valued loss runs
- Photographs of all insured structures

### 3.2 Coastal Tier 1 Supplement (New in v3.0)

- Wind mitigation inspection report, current within 3 years, required
  for all Tier 1 coastal risks regardless of roof age

## 4. Prohibited Risks

- Vacant buildings exceeding 25% of TIV
- Buildings with unrepaired prior fire or flood damage
- Risks located in a FEMA-declared flood zone without a current flood
  policy in force

## 5. Referral Triggers

Refer to home office underwriting when any of the following apply:

- TIV exceeds the applicable limit in Section 2
- Prior loss history shows two or more property losses exceeding
  $100,000 in the last five years
- Any risk requesting a manuscript endorsement
- Any Tier 1 coastal risk unable to produce the wind mitigation report
  required under Section 3.2

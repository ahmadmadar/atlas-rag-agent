# Cyber Liability Underwriting Addendum

**Effective:** April 1, 2025
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This addendum supplements the general underwriting guidelines with
eligibility, sublimit, and referral standards specific to cyber
liability risks, per the Risk Appetite Statement's cyber liability
capacity limit of $5,000,000 per occurrence.

## 2. Eligibility Requirements

An applicant must attest to all of the following to be eligible for a
cyber liability quote:

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforced on all remote access and
  email accounts
- Endpoint detection and response (EDR) software deployed on all
  workstations and servers
- Data backups tested for successful restoration at least quarterly,
  with at least one backup copy stored offline or immutable

## 3. Sublimits

- Ransomware/cyber extortion payment sublimit: **$1,000,000**
- Social engineering / fraudulent instruction sublimit: **$250,000**
- Business interruption waiting period: 8 hours

## 4. Required Application Supplement

All cyber liability submissions require the Atlas Cyber Supplemental
Application in addition to the standard ACORD application, including
network diagram and incident response plan summary.

## 5. Referral Triggers

Refer to home office underwriting when:

- The applicant cannot attest to MFA or EDR deployment (decline unless
  a compensating control is approved by home office)
- Requested limit exceeds $5,000,000 per occurrence
- Applicant has had a reportable breach or ransomware event in the
  prior 3 years

# Producer Compensation Policy

**Effective:** September 1, 2023
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs commission schedules, contingent compensation
eligibility, and licensing compliance requirements for independent
agents and brokers ("producers") appointed to place business with
Atlas Underwriting.

## 2. Base Commission Schedule by Line

- Commercial property: 15% new business, 12% renewal
- General liability: 15% new business, 12% renewal
- Cyber liability: 10% new business, 10% renewal
- Commercial auto: 12% new business, 10% renewal

## 3. Contingent / Profit-Sharing Commission Criteria

Producers are eligible for contingent (profit-sharing) commission when
all of the following are met in a given calendar year:

- Minimum $1,000,000 in bound gross written premium with Atlas
- Loss ratio for the producer's book below 55%
- Retention ratio above 80%

Contingent commission is capped at an additional 5% of gross written
premium.

## 4. Compliance Requirements

- Producers must maintain active Errors & Omissions (E&O) coverage of
  at least $1,000,000 per occurrence.
- Producers must hold a current, unrestricted insurance producer
  license in every state in which they place Atlas business.
- Lapses in E&O coverage or licensing suspend commission payments until
  cured.

## 5. Termination of Agency Agreements

Atlas may terminate an agency agreement with 90 days' written notice,
or immediately for cause (license revocation, fraud, or failure to
remit premium within 30 days of the due date).

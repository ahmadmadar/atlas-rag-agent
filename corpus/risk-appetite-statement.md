# Risk Appetite Statement

**Effective:** January 1, 2025 (reviewed annually; current cycle
approved for the 2026 underwriting year)
**Status:** Active

## 1. Purpose

This statement defines the lines of business, classes, and capacity
limits within which Atlas Underwriting will deploy capital, and the
classes it will not write regardless of pricing or terms.

## 2. In-Appetite Classes

- Commercial property (occupancy classes 1-6)
- General liability (commercial, non-habitational)
- Cyber liability (see Cyber Liability Underwriting Addendum for
  eligibility)
- Commercial auto (fleet, non-hazmat)

## 3. Prohibited / Excluded Classes

Atlas will not write:

- Habitational risks over 4 stories without a fully functioning
  automatic sprinkler system
- Cannabis cultivation, processing, or dispensary operations
- Adult entertainment establishments
- Risks located in a designated active wildfire evacuation zone
  without an approved defensible-space mitigation plan
- Standalone flood coverage (ceded entirely to the NFIP or approved
  private flood carriers)

## 4. Capacity Limits by Line

- Commercial property: $25,000,000 per location, $50,000,000 per
  named-storm event aggregate per county
- General liability: $2,000,000 per occurrence / $4,000,000 aggregate
- Cyber liability: $5,000,000 per occurrence

## 5. Annual Review Cycle

This statement is reviewed and re-approved by the Atlas Underwriting
Committee each October for the following calendar year. Interim
amendments require Chief Underwriting Officer sign-off.
